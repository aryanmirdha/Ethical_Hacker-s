Networking & Cybersecurity Basics

Networking is the foundation of cybersecurity. Every cyberattack, vulnerability, and security defense depends on networking concepts.

Students will learn IP addresses, DNS, TCP/IP, HTTP, HTTPS, ports, firewalls, routing, switching, and VPN technologies.

Cybersecurity basics include malware, phishing, social engineering, vulnerabilities, encryption, and authentication systems.

Practical labs will help students understand packet analysis and traffic monitoring using tools such as Wireshark and Nmap.

Students will also learn how attackers exploit weak network configurations and insecure services.

Understanding networking helps ethical hackers identify vulnerabilities and improve system security.

This section creates a strong technical foundation for penetration testing and advanced cybersecurity topics.

By the end of this module, students will understand how systems communicate and how security professionals analyze networks.
