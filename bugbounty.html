Bug Bounty Fundamentals

Bug bounty programs allow ethical hackers to find vulnerabilities in real-world applications and report them responsibly.

Students will learn reconnaissance, subdomain enumeration, vulnerability analysis, and responsible disclosure.

Practical exercises include asset discovery, scanning, JavaScript analysis, and directory brute-forcing.

Students will understand how professional bug bounty hunters identify attack surfaces and weak configurations.

The course also teaches professional report writing and impact analysis techniques.

Bug bounty hunting improves analytical thinking, patience, and technical research abilities.

Hands-on labs simulate real bug bounty environments and vulnerability discovery processes.

By the end of this module, students will understand how modern bug bounty programs operate.
