Privilege Escalation Techniques

Privilege escalation is the process of gaining higher-level access after compromising a system.

Students will learn Linux and Windows privilege escalation techniques used during penetration testing.

Topics include weak permissions, SUID binaries, kernel exploits, scheduled tasks, and service misconfigurations.

Students will use enumeration tools and manual analysis to identify privilege escalation opportunities.

Practical labs simulate real-world privilege escalation scenarios on vulnerable machines.

Learners will understand how attackers move from low-privileged access to administrative control.

Security hardening concepts are also introduced to help defend systems against attacks.

By the end of this module, students will gain advanced exploitation knowledge used in cybersecurity operations.
