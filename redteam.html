Red Teaming & Exploitation

Red teaming simulates real-world cyberattacks against organizations to test their defenses.

Students will learn reconnaissance, phishing simulations, exploitation, persistence, and lateral movement techniques.

The course introduces offensive security methodologies used by professional red team operators.

Learners will understand how attackers bypass defenses and evade security monitoring systems.

Practical labs simulate realistic attack scenarios against enterprise environments.

Students will also learn command-and-control concepts and post-exploitation operations.

This section focuses on advanced offensive security concepts and real-world attack methodologies.

By the end of this module, students will gain advanced red teaming and exploitation knowledge.
